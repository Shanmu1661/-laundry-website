How to make your favourite clothes last longer.

Priya, Laundry Care Expert 5 min read 6 comments
Best for Cotton, linen, and everyday wear
Main takeaway Gentle cycles and early stain care add years
LaundryPro tip Wash smaller loads so fabrics move freely

Great clothing is an investment in how you feel. Good care is the simplest way to honour it and keep every favourite piece ready for repeat wear.

Most damage happens through everyday habits rather than dramatic mistakes. A little more space in the drum, lower heat, and faster stain response can make common fabrics look fresher for far longer.

Read the label, then trust the basics.

Sort by colour and fabric, avoid overfilling the machine, and give delicate pieces room to breathe. Small routines make a remarkable difference over time, especially when garments are washed weekly.

Spot clean early.

Treat stains promptly with a fabric-safe method instead of escalating heat or harsh chemicals. When in doubt, bring special garments to a professional before the stain sets deeper into the fibres.

  • Turn printed or dark garments inside out before washing.
  • Use cold or lukewarm water unless the care label asks otherwise.
  • Skip high dryer heat for elastics, trims, and soft knits.
Frequent fading, stretching, and rough texture usually point to too much heat or friction, not poor fabric quality.
