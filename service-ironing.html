Ironing Only

Already washed? We'll press and finish your clothes to a crisp, wrinkle-free standard.

Sharp creases, smooth finishes.

Perfect when your clothes are clean but need a professional press. Our team uses steam and temperature settings matched to each fabric for a polished, ready-to-wear finish.

What's included

  • Steam pressing by fabric type
  • Crease setting for trousers and shirts
  • Hanger or fold delivery option
  • Final quality inspection

How it works

Hand over pre-washed garments → we press and finish → delivered back within 24 hours, crisp and ready to wear.

Finish options

Choose a light, regular or crisp finish when booking your pickup.
