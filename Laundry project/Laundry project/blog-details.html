CARE GUIDE · AUG 06, 2026

How to make your favourite clothes last longer.

By Priya, Laundry Care Expert · 6 comments

Great clothing is an investment in how you feel. Good care is the simplest way to honour it.

Read the label, then trust the basics.

Sort by colour and fabric, avoid overfilling the machine, and give delicate pieces room to breathe. Small routines make a remarkable difference over time.

Spot clean early.

Treat stains promptly with a fabric-safe method instead of escalating heat or harsh chemicals. When in doubt, bring special garments to a professional.
