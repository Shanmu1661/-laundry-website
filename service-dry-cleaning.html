Dry Cleaning

Expert care for suits, silk, wool and other delicate garments that need specialist treatment.

Preserve shape, colour and fabric integrity.

Dry cleaning uses solvent-based processes that protect delicate fibres. Ideal for formal wear, ethnic garments, tailored pieces and items labelled dry clean only.

What's included

  • Stain inspection and pre-treatment
  • Fabric-safe solvent cleaning
  • Professional pressing and finishing
  • Protective garment bag delivery

Best for

Suits, blazers, sarees, silk blouses, wool coats, lehengas and designer pieces that cannot be machine washed.

Before we clean

We inspect labels, trims and stains, then confirm any specialist treatment needed.

Care note

Tell us about older stains or fragile embellishments when you hand over your item.
